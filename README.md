# Solavisetech-women-in-data-cohort2
Women in Data Cohort 2 training repository

## About the Program
This two-month program is focused on developing women's skills, especially in the fields of data and technology.

## About Me
My name is Danielle Mabouanda, a young Cameroonian passionate about technology, software development, and data.  
I studied software engineering and have been working for more than three years as an IT specialist in a service company.  
Challenges, continuous learning, and problem-solving are among the things I enjoy most in the field of technology.

## My Expectations
I hope to improve my technical skills, gain practical experience, and further expand my network in the tech industry.

## What I Hope to Learn
I hope to gain a better understanding of Git, GitHub, Python, data analysis, and collaboration tools.  
I also want to better understand what the role and daily work of a woman in the data field truly involve.
